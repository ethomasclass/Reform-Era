# Gemini image prompts: Fix Everything

These are only for moments where no archival image exists. Real people always come from archival sources, so none of these prompts shows a recognizable real person.

## How to run them

- **Model:** Gemini image generation (Nano Banana Pro / `gemini-3-pro-image`), at the largest size it offers.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for every image.
- **Each prompt:** paste the whole block, including the **Style** paragraph. Keeping the style text identical is what makes the images look like one series.
- **Sending them back:** a PNG, named as shown (for example `ch02_anxious_bench.png`). If a result has text, extra fingers, modern objects or a wrong detail, regenerate it rather than fixing it.
- **Outlined subject (optional):** a few images end with a **Mask pass**. After you have an image you like, upload that same image back into Gemini with the mask-pass prompt and send me both files. It lets me put the orange-coral color and the teal outline on exactly the right figures.

**Style** (included at the end of every prompt below):

> Style: an American oil painting from the 1830s–1840s in the manner of the period's genre painters, such as George Caleb Bingham and William Sidney Mount. Muted earth palette (umber, ochre, slate blue, dull red), warm directional light, visible brushwork, faint craquelure, slightly aged varnish. Every detail must be historically accurate for the stated year and place: clothing, hairstyles, buildings, furniture, lamps and tools. No text of any kind: no letters, words, signs, labels, captions, signatures, dates or watermarks. No frame or border. No recognizable real historical person. Faces, where visible, belong to ordinary anonymous people.
